# Samba Brand Identity & Design System

## 1. Brand Essence: "Technical Warmth"
Samba's visual identity exists at the intersection of rigorous, high-precision data intelligence and organic, human narrative. It is where the "synthetic" (AI, code, structure) meets the "natural" (lifestyle, warmth, culture).

**Core Philosophy:**
> "Add data/tek to imagery."
> We do not just show data; we show how data *sees* the world. We layer intelligence over reality.

---

## 2. Typography
**Primary Typeface: Season**
The typographic voice of Samba is defined by the **Season** family, which perfectly embodies the brand's dual nature through its "Mix" (Serif/Display) and "Sans" variants.

### **A. Season Mix (The "Voice")**
Used for Headlines, Hero Statements, and Editorial touches. It carries the "human" and "warm" side of the brand with its serif characteristics.
*   **Usage:** Large titles, key insights, emotional hooks.
*   **Weights:**
    *   `Season Mix Light`: For elegant, airy headers.
    *   `Season Mix Regular/Medium`: For primary impact statements.

### **B. Season Sans (The "Brain")**
Used for UI elements, Data Visualization, Body Copy, and Technical labeling. It represents the "machine" and "precision."
*   **Usage:** Navigation, buttons, charts, code snippets, "micro-typography" (timestamps, coordinates).
*   **Weights:**
    *   `Season Sans Regular`: Body text (legibility).
    *   `Season Sans Medium/SemiBold`: Buttons, navigational links, key data points.

### **Typography Principles**
*   **Hierarchy:** Extreme contrast. Pair massive *Season Mix* headlines with tiny, precise *Season Sans* technical labels.
*   **Micro-Labels:** Treat text as texture. Use small caps, coordinates (e.g., `X: 1040 Y: 447`), and timestamps (`15:07:45`) to decorate images, mimicking a machine's HUD (Heads-Up Display).

## 3. Color Palette

### **Primary Backgrounds (The Canvas)**
*   **Void Black:** `#050505` (Deep, infinite space for data to glow).
*   **Stark White:** `#FFFFFF` (Clean, editorial space for clarity).
*   **Usage:** Use seamless transitions between Black and White slides to signal a shift from "Concept/Theory" (White) to "Deep Tech/Execution" (Black).

### **Accent Colors (The "Signal")**
*   **Acid Green:** (e.g., `#CCFF00` or similar electric lime).
    *   *Role:* The "active" state. Used for cursors, highlight text, primary buttons, and live data spikes.
*   **Signal Red/Orange:**
    *   *Role:* Alert states or human warmth accents in photography.

---

## 4. Graphic Elements & Imagery

### **A. The "Data/Tek" Layer**
These elements represent the AI's logic and structure.
*   **Wireframes:** Thin white lines (0.5pt - 1pt) depicting 3D structures (globes, cubes) or UI skeletons.
*   **The "Knowledge Graph":** Nodes and edges connecting disjointed images or concepts.
*   **HUD Overlays:**
    *   Bounding boxes around objects in photos.
    *   Tracking dots on key features.
    *   Terminal code blocks floating in space (`1401 AUTOCODER SYSTEM`).

### **B. The "Human" Layer**
*   **Cinematic Photography:** Candid, lifestyle imagery with warm lighting. Avoid "stock" looks.
*   **Rolling Cards:** Images presented as decks or carousels (e.g., Apple Cover Flow style or 3D spiraling cards).
*   **2.5D Depth:** Images are rarely flat; they float, tilt, or overlap to create a sense of deep space.

---

## 5. Layout System
*   **"Space to Breathe":** 40-50% of the canvas should be negative space.
*   **Grid Alignment:** Rigid alignment for text and UI elements to maintain the "software" feel.
*   **Split Compositions:**
    *   **50/50:** Left side for Typography (Story), Right side for Visuals (Evidence).
*   **Containerization:** Use rounded-corner cards (radius ~8px-12px) to house disparate content types (e.g., "Reviews," "Code," "Profiles").

---

## 6. Motion Behaviors (Visual Reasoning)
*   **Snap & Lock:** Elements shouldn't just "fade" in; they should snap into grid positions like a mechanical shutter or a data block loading.
*   **Continuous Flow:** Horizontal scrolling or "infinite canvas" effects that connect one idea to the next.
*   **Scanning:** Subtle animations of lines scanning across images or data points pulsating to imply active processing.

---

## 7. "Forever" Slide Checklist
When creating any new asset, ask:
1.  **Is the font correct?** (Season Mix for feeling, Season Sans for facts).
2.  **Is there "Tek" on the "Img"?** Have I layered a data element (grid, crosshair, label) over the photography?
3.  **Is it clean?** Is there enough black/white space?
4.  **Is there an accent?** Is there a touch of Acid Green to guide the eye?
